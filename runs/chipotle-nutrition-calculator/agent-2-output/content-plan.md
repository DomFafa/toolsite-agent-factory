## Education Sections

### How It Works

Explain that the tool adds published ingredient nutrition values and lets users adjust portions.

### Bowl Vs Burrito Calories

Explain that the burrito tortilla is the main structural difference and adds a large calorie/carb load.

### High-Protein Orders

Suggest chicken, double chicken, steak, beans, and fajita vegetables as common high-protein planning levers.

### Lower-Calorie Swaps

Suggest bowl/salad formats, light rice, extra salsa, lettuce, fajita vegetables, and cautious use of queso, sour cream, cheese, guac, chips, and vinaigrette.

### Accuracy And Portions

Explain that published data uses standard portions and actual restaurant scoops vary.

## FAQ Draft

Q: Is this the official Chipotle calculator?

A: No. This is an independent tool that estimates nutrition from published menu data.

Q: How accurate is it?

A: It should match standard published portions reasonably well, but actual restaurant servings can vary.

Q: Can I calculate light or extra portions?

A: Yes. Selected items can be adjusted to light, normal, extra, or double portions.

Q: What adds the most calories?

A: Tortillas, chips, guacamole, queso, sour cream, cheese, and vinaigrette can quickly raise totals.

Q: Does this store my meal?

A: No. The calculator runs locally in your browser and does not require login.
