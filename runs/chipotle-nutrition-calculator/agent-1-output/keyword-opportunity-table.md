## Decision Notes

- The primary keyword is validated but difficult.
- The best SEO strategy is a strong calculator homepage plus long-tail pages/sections for meal types, portions, and diet presets.
- Portion modifiers are the clearest product gap found during research.
- The build should prioritize utility quality before content volume.

## Handoff To Agent 2

Agent 2 should use the primary keyword for positioning, but the brief should require a keyword cluster rather than a single-page exact-match SEO page.
